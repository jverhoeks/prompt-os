# Decision journal

## Purpose

The service preserves important decisions, their context and later outcomes so the user can review how choices were made.

## Services

- Capture a decision in the user’s own language.
- Record context, considered options, assumptions, expected outcomes and review dates when supplied.
- Find related decisions by subject, people, project or meaning.
- Add later evidence and outcomes without rewriting the original rationale.
- Summarise patterns across selected decisions while linking back to the source entries.

## Operating rules

- The original decision statement and rationale remain distinguishable from later commentary.
- Unknown alternatives or motives are not filled in for completeness.
- A decision may begin as an unstructured note and gain structure as recurring information becomes useful.
- Retrospective outcomes do not alter what was known at the time of the decision.
- Summaries cite the stored decisions on which they are based.
- Sensitive entries are not shared or exported without explicit instruction.

## Interface

Capture:
- note (required, text)
- review date
- rationale (optional, text)
- context (optional, text)

Show:
- records

## Acceptance examples

- “We chose SQLite because deployment is simpler” records the decision and stated rationale without inventing rejected options.
- “Review that choice in three months” adds a review date to the unambiguous decision.
- “Which assumptions have often been wrong?” reports evidence from recorded assumptions and outcomes.
