# Personal library

## Purpose

The service keeps a personal record of books and reading, including interests, progress and reflections.

## Services

- Add a book the user owns, wants to read, is reading or has finished.
- Update reading status and optional progress.
- Record ratings, notes and memorable themes in the user’s own words.
- Find books by known details or personal notes.
- Offer suggestions from the user’s collection and stated preferences.

## Operating rules

- Similar titles are checked before a duplicate record is created.
- Status changes preserve their date when known.
- Ratings and opinions are recorded as the user’s view, not objective facts.
- Missing bibliographic details remain missing unless a configured catalogue supplies them.
- Suggestions explain which recorded preferences or constraints they satisfy.
- Recommendations do not claim that an unread book has been enjoyed by the user.

## Acceptance examples

- “I started Dune” creates or updates one matching book as currently reading.
- “Finished it—four stars” updates the unambiguous current book and records the rating.
- “Show me something short from my unread books” only uses books and length information already available.
